Sources & Architecture

ASTRA orchestrates multiple NASA scientific APIs and IBM watsonx.ai to deliver real-time space situational intelligence through natural language.

NASA DONKI SWRC
api.nasa.gov/DONKI/FLR

Space Weather Research Center database. Provides solar flare classifications (A–X), geomagnetic storm events, and real-time space weather alerts.

NASA NeoWs CNEOS
api.nasa.gov/neo/rest/v1/feed

Near Earth Object Web Service. Tracks near-Earth asteroid close approaches, miss distances, and potential hazard classifications.

NASA / JPL SBDB CAD API
ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/cad.api

JPL Small-Body Database close-approach data API. Covers comet and minor-planet proximity events, approach velocities, and orbital parameters.

IBM watsonx.ai Granite
ibm.com/watsonx

IBM Granite foundation models handle natural language understanding, intent classification, LLM tool-calling orchestration, and synthesis of final answers from scientific data.
